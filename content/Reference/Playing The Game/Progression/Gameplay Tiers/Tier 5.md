## Paragon Tier

</div>
The Paragon Tier is the highest designation among Channelers — the mark of a truly generational name, known not just across the world but across time. Paragon Badges are small vessels forged from cosmic materials, carrying exceedingly rare Prisma that borders on pure Prima, tinted only by the faintest hue of the bearer's own soul. No Guild registry tracks them. No introduction is required. The name arrives before the person does.

Paragon Channelers serve as the platonic ideal of their Archetype — so completely that their unique journey and relationship with Prisma often forms the foundation of the spellbooks and fighting styles practiced by aspiring Channelers for generations after. Each Archetype is associated with at least one Paragon whose path became the map others follow.

Paragons tend to reside in one of two places: the most prestigious and powerful kingdoms in the world, which court them as living symbols of national identity — or small, unremarkable villages that find themselves suddenly and permanently on the map as the birthplace of a legend. The distance between Virtuoso and Paragon is not a gap so much as a threshold of a different kind entirely. Most continents count their living Paragons on one hand.

**Paragon-tier threats are named.** They are the kind that do not merely endanger settlements or reshape regions, but they threaten the continuity of the age itself, and their defeat or containment becomes the event that future generations use to mark time. These Channelers tend to conclude their journey not with a personal achievement, but a civilizational one: a new fighting style that rewrites how a weapon is understood, a category of spells that didn't exist before they imagined it, or a technology that permanently changes what the world believes is possible.
