Morphic Biomes are environments where **Flow** is the dominant natural force - resulting in the large majority of the region's Prisma being [[Morphic]] in nature.

### Commonly Morphic Biomes

Morphic Biomes are largely considered to be _dynamic_ and _aquatic_ natural environments, where the weather, geography, flora, and fauna are malleable and follow ongoing currents of change.

**The commonly cited regions are as follows:** <br/>
Oceans <br/>
Coastlines <br/>
Rivers <br/>
Restless Lakes <br/>

**The weather patterns and phenomena can also be a large insight as to the Morphic nature of a particular region, these being:** <br/>
Thunderstorms <br/>
Heavy Wind <br/>
Moving Water <br/>
Heavy Deposits of [[Animite#Tidestone|Tidestone]] <br/>

Morphic Biomes - much like the signature Morphic Prisma - serve to flow around anything that enters them, often sweeping travelers into their ongoing currents and flow.
