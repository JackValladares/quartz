A Prisma's Inflection describes the changes imposed upon it by _abstract ideas and concepts_. Inflection exists on top of core [[Prismatic Expression]] - modulating and twisting its core shape and properties, but never replacing them. Not all Prisma has a distinct inflection; untouched wilderness tends to represent raw Expressions.

## What is Prismatic Inflection?

Prisma is [[Prismatic Stain|Stained]] in _two_ different ways: <br/>
First - by objective, material, _worldly_ phenomena (granting its [[Expression]]) <br/>
Second - by subjective, immaterial, _abstract_ phenomena (granting its **Inflection**) <br/>

This second-order method of staining occurs when sentient, opinionated, biased beings impose those very ideas on nearby Prisma.

While all Prisma has some form of Expression in tandem with the world's objective, unchanging, fundamental forces, Prisma only takes on inflection from sentient, biased, subjective phenomena. 

Prismatic Inflection fades slowly when a culture is removed from a region, and quickly when detached from a living being's soul. Thus, a handy heuristic is as follows:

The more potent the Inflection of a region's Prisma, the more likely it is that the region is home to civilization. The Prisma of True Wilderness is wholly represented by its Expression, while bustling kingdoms have a deep inflection tied to the surrounding Prisma.
