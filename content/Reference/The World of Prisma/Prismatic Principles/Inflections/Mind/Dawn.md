### Dawn as a Concept
The Concept of *Dawn* represents a deep reverence for the sun and its warmth. Those who represent Dawn are bright and spirited - wearing their heart on their sleeves,

This ideal arose during the world's *Era of Shade*, where Vampires, Werewolves, and other creatures of the night rose to prominence. Such fiends were warded away by the sunlight of the dawn, and struck down by spirited warriors of light wielding mighty smites. As such, the Dawn itself became a nigh-religious ideal in many cultures - and Entropic, Stratic, and Morphic Prisma were given a *Bright* [[Prismatic Inflection|Inflection]] to match.
