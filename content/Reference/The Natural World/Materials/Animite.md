A category of ores that act as potent natural [[Lens|Lenses]]  in their environments representing their corresponding [[Prismatic Expression]]. Animite is a foundational component in an Artifice utilizing [[Prisma]].

## Prominent Types of Animite

### Lower Order Animite

The most abundant and well-documented Animite are those stained by the World's three fundamental forces. **Loamstone**, **Cinderstone**, and **Tidestone** - each a product of [[Stratic|Creation]], [[Entropic|Destruction]], and [[Morphic|Change]] respectively - are found wherever their corresponding force holds dominance, and are collectively considered the foundation of practical Prismatic craft. Their prevalence, combined with the reliability of their staining properties, is widely believed to be one of the reasons Lower Order Expressions are considered fundamental in the first place.

### Higher Order Animite

Where Lower Order Animite emerge from the World's most ancient and pervasive forces, Higher Order Animite are rarer by nature — products of environments where two forces act in equal and sustained measure. For centuries, Composite Expressions were understood primarily as a product of artifice and refinement rather than something the World produced on its own terms.

The discovery of the [[Prismatic Wilds]] complicated that assumption considerably. In these anomalous regions, where competing forces have reached a kind of equilibrium, the World has begun producing native Higher Order Animite — stones that bear Composite Expression without any intervention at all. They remain poorly understood, and rarer still outside the Wilds themselves.
