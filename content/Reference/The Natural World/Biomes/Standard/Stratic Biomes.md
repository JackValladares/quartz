Stratic Biomes are environments where **Iteration** is the dominant natural force - resulting in the large majority of the region's Prisma being [[Stratic]] in nature.

### Commonly Stratic Biomes
Stratic Biomes are largely considered to be *ancient* and *layered* natural environments, where the weather, geography, flora, and fauna are dense and built upon aeons of organic deposit.


**The commonly cited regions are as follows:**
Forests
Plains
Mountains
Glaciers

**The weather patterns and phenomena can also be a large insight as to the Stratic nature of a particular region, these being:**
Temperate
Still
Ongoing deposits of Earth and Stone


Stratic Biomes - much like the signature Blue Prisma - serve to patiently integrate and build upon themselves with anything that enters them.
