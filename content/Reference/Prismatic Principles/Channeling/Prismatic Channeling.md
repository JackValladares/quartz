The art of using and manipulating Prisma – both external and from within – is known as **Channeling**.

The term is a very broad umbrella that covers a wide range of applications such as spellcasting, artifice, alchemy, capture, storage, and even meditation.

## What is Channeling?
**If Prisma is moved, manipulated, imbued, or spent – it is being _channeled_.**

Because the bodies and souls of [[Prisma#Living Beings as Prismatic Beings|Living Beings]] are imbued with Prisma, **Prisma can be channeled using the self as a focus**. One’s own Prisma can be Stained and molded by their intent and then imbued into their body or evoked into the world in the form of powerful [[Prismatic Spells|Spells]] and Abilities.

Deeper understanding of oneself and one’s own Prisma makes the process more efficient and potent, where more advanced Channelers are able to shape their Prisma in increasingly complex and sophisticated ways; twisting their Prisma output to just the right color and nature for specific use-cases. Novice-level channelers, in contrast, waste large amounts of Prisma attempting to do the same.

**Prisma is also channeled through external artifice** depending on the context and goals.

These external means of Channeling Prisma might take the form of spellcasting focuses that assist in the process of staining one’s own Prisma. This might also be represented by Gear powered by one’s own Prisma output such as weapons, armor, and vehicles. **The spectrum of Prismatic Engineering is as vast as it is useful**, and throughout the broader world ranges from simple household appliances and Prismatic Batteries to sprawling kingdoms with sophisticated Prismatic Infrastructure and War Machines.

Those who choose to immerse themselves in lifelong pursuit of Prismatic Understanding - living exceptional lives of expertise and a deep exploration of self - are referred to as **Channelers**. The different classes of Channelers, such as a Mage who uses Prisma to cast powerful spells or an Artificer who uses it to enchant armor and weapons, are referred to as **Archetypes**.

Therefore, **every channeler belongs to some kind of Archetype describing their general skillset.**

The first, most fundamental step to becoming a Channeler is figuring out the nature of one’s own Body and Soul. That is – their own Prismatic makeup, as unique as fingerprints upon one’s own fingers. Understanding your Prisma allows you to understand your strengths and weaknesses as a Channeler.
