**Prima given form is called Prisma**. The word itself is a useful shorthand for this concept – where a type of Prisma represents Prima of a particular nature.

## Energy Given Expression
The nature of Prisma nature is obtained through a process called a [[Prismatic Stain]]. Prismatic Staining occurs whenever Prima interacts with the world and the world, in turn, leaves its mark. 

	A volcanic shelf saturates the Prima above it with heat and force.

	A glacial basin imposes its patience and weight.

	A coastal storm charges the air with restless, shifting energy. 

Prima absorbs all of it indifferently, taking on the character of whatever surrounds it.

Because every interaction with the world affects the properties of Prisma, no two samples are identical. All Prisma is technically unique, being endlessly stained and molded by the world around it. However, past a certain point these differences are arbitrary and exist on a spectrum that can be divided into different functional categories, which are known as forms of [[Prismatic Expression]].
