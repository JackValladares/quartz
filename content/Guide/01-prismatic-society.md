As has been established - [[Prisma]] is as transformative as it is plentiful; imbuing the world and its denizens alike with raw energy ripe for the taking.

Naturally, it would follow that the societies and cultures of the world harness and study Prisma just like any other natural phenomenon - leading to robust applications in engineering, spellcasting, infrastructure, and day-to-day life. 

Just as one would call this same principle applied to ores *metallurgy* and life *biology*, when applied to Prisma we get the field of **Channeling**.
